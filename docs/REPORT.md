# Phase 1 report — Experimental baseline for BRD4-BD1 ligand complexes

**Status:** experimental measurements only. No predicted structures, no docking,
no prospective screening. All numbers below are generated by
`python -m brd4_analysis.cli all` and are traceable to `results/` CSV/JSON
outputs and the SHA-256 fingerprinted inputs under `data/`.

**Reference:** Vidler et al. (2013), *Discovery of Novel Small-Molecule
Inhibitors of BRD4 Using Structure-Based Virtual Screening*, J. Med. Chem.,
doi:10.1021/jm4011302.

**Ligand identification.** The bound inhibitor is distinguished from
crystallisation additives and water by component ID. 4MEN contains the

inhibitor 25V plus EDO, dimethyl sulfoxide (DMS) and 194 waters. For both

template reproduces the CCD canonical isomeric SMILES exactly
(`identity_check.level = "smiles_match"` in `results/summary.json`), so
chemical identity and protonation/formal-charge state are verified, not
assumed. Neither ligand has a stereogenic centre; the 25V quinoline
nitrogen is present as the N-oxide `[n+][O-]` (net neutral).

## 3. Experimentally observed contacts (heavy-atom geometry only)

A residue is "in contact" at threshold *t* iff its minimum ligand
heavy-atom distance ≤ *t*. No hydrogen-bond or interaction-type
classification is made at this stage.

| Threshold | 4MEN residues | 4MEO residues |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 3.5 Å | 5 | 5 |
| ≤ 4.0 Å | 11 | 11 |
| ≤ 5.0 Å | 13 | 14 |

Closest contacts (`contact_pairs_*.csv`):

- **4MEN:** Asn140 ND2–N2 of the triazolopyrimidine, 2.84 Å; then the
  hydrophobic shelf Phe83 (3.35), Trp81 (3.42), Leu92 (3.47), Pro82 (3.49).
- **4MEO:** Asp88 backbone N–N-oxide O1, 2.67 Å; Asn140 ND2–acetamide
  carbonyl O, 2.88 Å; Leu92 (2.91), Trp81 (3.34), Val87 (3.45).

### Interpretation (kept deliberately conservative)

- Both chemotypes converge on **Asn140**, the conserved acetyl-lysine
  recognition residue of BD1: the 25K triazolopyrimidine ring N and the
  25V acetamide carbonyl both sit at canonical H-bond distance (~2.85 Å)
  of Asn140 ND2 — two different acceptor chemistries serving the same
  anchoring role.
- Both ligands pack against the **WPF shelf** (Trp81/Pro82/Phe83) and the
  Leu92/Val87 rim, i.e. the known lipophilic walls of the acetyl-lysine
  pocket.
- In 4MEO the quinoline **N-oxide oxygen sits 2.67 Å from the Asp88
  backbone amide N** — geometrically consistent with a backbone
  NH···O(N-oxide) hydrogen bond; this is the feature that differentiates
  the 25V binding mode.
- Shared ligand-protein neighbourhoods despite Tanimoto = 0.154 quantify
  the scaffold-hopping point of Vidler et al.: distinct chemotypes
  reproduce the same contact topology.

## 4. Limitations

1. **Heavy-atom geometry only.** At 1.72–1.81 Å resolution X-ray gives no
   H positions; distances near 2.7–3.4 Å are *consistent with* hydrogen
   bonds but no angle/protonation criteria were applied. Coordinate
   uncertainty (~0.1–0.2 Å) is not propagated into the contact flags.
2. **Waters excluded from contact counting.** 192/194 waters are present
   and water-mediated contacts are real in BRD4; they are deliberately
   out of scope for this baseline.
3. **Single model, single ligand pose, occupancy not analysed.** Both
   entries are single-model X-ray structures; alternative conformers, if
   any, are not considered.
4. **Descriptors are CCD-based**, i.e. idealised chemistry; pKa/tautomer
   states are as deposited and were verified only against the PDB
   records, not re-derived.
5. **Two structures ≠ statistics.** This baseline quantifies geometry for
   these two complexes only; it is a reference for Phase 2 comparison,
   not a binding model.

## 5. Relevance to early hit identification

The baseline pins down the measurable signature a prospective BRD4-BD1
hit must reproduce: (i) an acceptor anchored to Asn140 at ~2.8–2.9 Å,
(ii) packing against the WPF shelf within ~5 Å, (iii) fragment-like
properties (MW < 300, TPSA < 60, cLogP < 3.5). These are exactly the
quantities a structure-prediction or virtual-screening method should
recover before any ranking claim is meaningful — the comparison point
for Phase 2.
